A research instrument,
not a money machine.

The agent observes returns, volatility, momentum, position and drawdown. Its spikes produce BUY, HOLD or SELL. Reward is based on net portfolio return after transaction costs and a drawdown penalty.

Passing this simulation does not predict live profitability. Before IBKR paper trading, the same policy must pass walk-forward tests on unseen historical data, survivorship-safe universes and stress regimes.
